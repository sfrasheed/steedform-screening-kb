## OR-40 · Print Construction is 99% blank — populate, or amend the rule?

**The column is not neglected. It is maintained differently than the rule assumes.**

| Value | Rows |
|---|---|
| `Face-printed` | **5** |
| **blank** | **387** |
| `Full body` | **0** |

The five populated rows are **exactly** the five materials this knowledge base names as face-printed — Kaya Como · Kaya Eclipse · Kaya Sienna · Zenith Ottoman Grey · Zenith Rosè. Whoever maintains the column uses it as a **positive marker**: populate when face-printed, leave blank otherwise. `[R-PROFILE v4]` reads blank as **unknown** and fails closed. **Both are reasonable. They are not compatible.**

⛔ **As written, 387 of 392 materials send every pencil-profile job to Needs Review.** The screen would look broken from its first day, and the fault would not be the rule.

**The question:** which one moves?

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Populate `Full body` on the other 387** | Blank becomes meaningful again. The rule works exactly as designed, and a **genuinely unknown** material still fails closed. **Recommended** — it is a bulk edit, and it keeps the safety property |
| **Amend `[R-PROFILE v4]` so blank means full body** | One line, no data work. But it removes the fail-closed guard, and a **new face-printed range added without being marked** would be priced with a pencil — which is precisely QU-58883 |

⛔ **This is a Register change if it goes the second way**, not a board change — `[R-PROFILE v4]` is a LAW, and amending it needs a dated version bump and propagation, not an edit in `05`.

**Interim, and it is an interim:** flag a pencil profile on a blank-construction material as reviewable, and **say why in the flag** — *"print construction not recorded"*, **never** *"may be face-printed"*. The first is true; the second invents a finding.

## OR-41 · Site-readiness scores on two entirely empty columns

| Column | Populated |
|---|---|
| **Client Rating (A/B/C)** | **0 / 188** |
| **Rating — Measure-ready** | **0 / 188** |
| **Rating — Install-ready** | **0 / 188** |
| Rating — Payment | 109 / 188 |

**OR-14 was ruled on 3 September 2026: site-readiness is the worst of Measure-ready and Install-ready.** Both columns are empty. The composite Client Rating is empty too. **In practice the A/B/C rating is payment-only** — A-VIP 15 · A 16 · B 53 · C 25.

That is not a criticism of the rubric. The A/B/C standard says plainly it was *"built criteria-first because the data doesn't exist yet."* But the audit it was written to enable **still has not happened**, and a screen cannot score site-readiness on nothing.

**The question:** does site-readiness **drop out of the composite** until the two columns hold data, or do the columns get populated first?

⛔ **What must not happen is the third option nobody chooses deliberately:** the screen scores an empty column as *good* because nothing came back. **An empty read is not a pass** `[R-READ v1]`. Until this is ruled, site-readiness must be reported as **not scored**, and the composite must say so rather than quietly running on Payment alone and calling it a rating.

## OR-38 · Is Screening Philosophy retired as a document?

**The consequential half of this is CLOSED.** The owner's note *"all stone to follow same rules"* was reviewed against `[R-D5 v6]` (the version current that day) and **the material-dependent thresholds were re-confirmed, not flattened.**

> Engineered and marble **laminate**. That is why they polish to a 40mm lamination and facet from **60mm**, while porcelain, sintered and hard natural — which cannot laminate — facet from **40mm**. The thresholds are lamination physics, not policy. Flattening them would promise a polished 40mm curve on granite that cannot be made: the exact mis-sell `R-D5` exists to prevent, and the one its own history records happening.

**What remains** is only the document question: the owner also wrote *"screening philosophy now redundant"*. The distance-from-optimum lens is currently carried in `03-risk-scoring.md` §4 as an **explanatory** lens that expressly does not score.

**Precise question:** is Screening Philosophy retired outright, or kept as a training document with no operative role?

**Low stakes either way.** It scores nothing, and the ladder it defers to is now stated. The only cost of leaving it is that a reader may mistake a lens for a scorer — which is why §4 carries a warning saying it is not one.

## OR-26 · No board identifier verified — DEFERRED

**The owner has ruled this not needed now:** *"we don't need connection to boards right now."* It is **deferred, not resolved**, and stays recorded because the finding does not go away by being postponed.

**What was established, 3 September 2026.** Authenticated against the SteedForm monday account as the account owner, all six boards this knowledge base depends on were queried by ID with `state: all`. **Every query returned an empty array.** There is no *SteedForm CRM* workspace in that account.

**What was NOT established:** that the identifiers are wrong. Either the CRM boards live in a **different account** from the one the connector reaches, or the IDs are **dead**. Neither was ruled out.

**Why it still matters when the app is built.** `[R-REF v2]` requires reference data to be read from the board on **every** screen — material class, print construction, appliance QC status, customer tier, service-area zone. **A screen that cannot reach a board cannot satisfy R-REF**, and `[R-READ v1]` applies at full scale: an empty result is not proof of absence until the read itself is proven.

**Until then:** every identifier in `08-board-and-column-registry.md` is **documentary, not verified**, and that document carries the warning at its head. Treat an empty read on any board named there as a finding, never a clean result.

## Closing an item

1. Decide.
2. Write it into `01-rule-register.md` as a new rule or a version bump, dated and attributed.
3. Propagate to every document in that rule's `Carried by` line, **updating the version tag** — a carrier on the old version is stale by definition.
4. Delete the entry here.
5. Re-screen every open lead the change affects `[R-RESCREEN v1]`.
